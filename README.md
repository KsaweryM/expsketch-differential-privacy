# Differential privacy techniques in data sketches

Master's thesis by Ksawery Możdżyński at Wrocław University of Science and Technology, supervised by dr inż. Jakub Lemiesz.

The thesis gives an overview of how differential privacy is applied to data sketches, the compact probabilistic summaries used to estimate statistics such as cardinality over large data streams. It proposes a differentially private version of the (Fast)ExpSketch algorithm and proves its privacy guarantees. The thesis also simulates a reconstruction attack on the sketch and analyzes the algorithm experimentally. The compiled thesis is in [`thesis.pdf`](thesis.pdf).
